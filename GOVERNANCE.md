# Project Governance

## Overview
The **Arduino-Projects** repository, managed by **QMX Corporation**, operates under an open, community-driven governance model. Unlike projects with a Benevolent Dictator For Life (BDFL), decisions, direction, and project maintenance are guided through collective consensus and collaborative contributions.

---

## Roles and Responsibilities

### 1. Contributors
* **Who they are:** Anyone who submits a bug report, documentation fix, code patch, or a new Arduino project via a Pull Request.
* **Responsibilities:** Follow the project's code of conduct, adhere to the coding standards (`.clang-format`, `.editorconfig`), and ensure their code is properly documented.

### 2. Maintainers
* **Who they are:** Active contributors who have demonstrated long-term commitment, deep understanding of the codebase, and a collaborative mindset.
* **Responsibilities:**
  * Review Pull Requests (PRs) and provide constructive feedback.
  * Merge approved contributions into the main branch.
  * Manage Issues, triage bug reports, and update project milestones or roadmaps.
  * Update the project ranking (`Rank.txt`) and oversee community events or challenges (`CONCURSE.txt`).

---

## Decision-Making Process
Decisions regarding the project's architecture, major features, tooling, or rule updates are made transparently through community discussion:

1. **Proposal:** Any contributor or maintainer can open an Issue or a discussion thread to propose a change or new feature.
2. **Discussion:** The community has open space to debate, test, and suggest improvements.
3. **Consensus:** Major changes require a consensus among active maintainers. If a vote is necessary, a simple majority of active maintainers decides the outcome. There is no single individual veto.

---

## Conflict Resolution
If conflicts arise among community members or maintainers:
* Discussions must remain respectful, technical, and focused on the project's goals.
* If a deadlock occurs, active maintainers will vote on the best path forward based on technical merit and community benefit.
